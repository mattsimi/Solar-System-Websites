MARS

About Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet because of its rusty colour. Scientists believe Mars once had water, which makes it interesting for future exploration.

Key Facts

  • Fourth planet from the Sun
  • Cold and dusty
  • Has the tallest volcano in the solar system

Fun Facts

  • Mars has two small moons
  • Strong dust storms can cover the planet
  • Robots explore Mars for signs of life

Planet Connection

Mars and Earth both have seasons, but Mars is colder because it is farther from the Sun and has a thinner atmosphere.

Did You Know?

Mars has seasons similar to Earth.

Quick Quiz

Q1: Why is Mars red?

Answer: Because of iron dust

Q2: Which planet is colder: Earth or Mars?

Answer: Mars

Q3: Why do scientists study Mars so closely?

Answer: To look for signs of past life and water
