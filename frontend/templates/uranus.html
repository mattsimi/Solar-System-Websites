URANUS

About Uranus

Uranus is unique because it tilts on its side, making its seasons extreme. It appears blue due to gases in its atmosphere.

Key Facts

  • Spins sideways
  • Blue-green colour
  • Very cold

Fun Facts

  • Coldest planet
  • Long seasons
  • Gas giant

Planet Connection

Uranus is different from all other planets because it spins on its side, while Earth and most planets spin upright.

Did You Know?

Uranus takes 84 Earth years to orbit the Sun.
