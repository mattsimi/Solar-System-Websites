MERCURY

About Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and the one closest to the Sun. Because it is so close, Mercury experiences extreme temperatures, becoming very hot during the day and extremely cold at night.

Key Facts

  • Closest planet to the Sun
  • No atmosphere to trap heat
  • One year on Mercury is very short

Fun Facts

  • A day on Mercury is longer than its year
  • Temperatures can change dramatically
  • Mercury has many craters, similar to the Moon

Planet Connection

Mercury is closest to the Sun, while Neptune is the farthest. This is why Mercury is very hot during the day, and Neptune is extremely cold.

Did You Know?

Mercury has no air to breathe, which means sound cannot travel there.

Quick Quiz

Q1: Is Mercury hotter during the day or at night?

Answer: During the day

Q2: Which planet is closer to the Sun: Mercury or Earth?

Answer: Mercury

Q3: Does Mercury have an atmosphere like Earth?

Answer: No
